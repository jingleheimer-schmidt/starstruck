


# Mod Descriptions

## base
The base Factorio game.

## UwUbe
Makes the Wube logo go UwU

## Disco Thrusters
Bring the party to your spaceship with disco thrusters that light up in all the colors of the rainbow.

## Rainbow Menu Logos
Recolor the factorio logo in the main menu to rainbow (like the official factorio discord), a single color of the rainbow or one of the provided pride flags. Adds a research in game to make the logos craftable and placeable. Supports Space Age.

## Preserve Products Produced
Preserves "Products finished" stat on machines when they are upgraded. Also preserves turret damage dealt and kills.

## Rainbow Lasers
Makes you laser beams RGB!
Works on laser turrets and peronal laser defenses.

## Nicer Fuel Glow for Space Age
Adds a subtle tint to the glow of burner entities depending on what fuel they're using.
Reupload of the Nicer Fuel Glow mod by FlamingCarrot and later fixes of wretlaw120

## Heat Glow
Prettier heat glow

## Disco Science
Science labs light up with the colours of the science packs they are consuming, instead of just blue.

## Milestones
Keep track of your progress in a fun way by finding out how fast you created key items.
Look back on your factory's history, compare with your friends, or challenge yourself.
Supports any list of items and comes with presets for popular big mods.
Can be added to existing games.

## Better Victory Screen
Upon victory it shows various interesting game statistics, like the total amount of time you spent hand crafting and your peak power production. Can be added to an existing save.

## Brake Squeal
Makes trains squeal/screech as they brake, dynamically changing based on speed. Auditory stimulation for the discerning engineer.

## Automatic Underground Pipe Connectors
Automatically place a pipe when two undergrounds are placed at a corner or one space apart.

## Filter Helper
Adds context aware quick select for filter inserters.

## Inserter Throughput
Displays the throughput of the selected inserter (under ideal conditions).
Now with a measuring tool for live testing.

## Dirt Path
Heavily trafficked areas will change over time.

## Mining Scars
Depleting ore patches causes significant scarring of the map.

## RP Item Anarchy
Creates item sprite variations for all items so you can watch individual iron plates move through the factory

## Pick It Up
When mining an inserter, also mine an item it dropped on the ground before-hand. Miniloader support.

## trainsaver
A dynamic screensaver that follows trains as they drive around

## Glowing Trees
Adds a glowing aura to the trees, and bioluminescence for foliage and decoratives.

## Subtle Lighting
Modifies the intensity of lamps, creating a more gradual falloff to the darkness beyond

## Cutscene Creator
Adds a command to create custom cutscenes. 

Use /cutscene and shift-click anywhere on the map to specify waypoints. 

## Leave The Lights On
Your vehicle's headlights stay on after you exit

## Visible Planets in Space
Render the local planet behind platforms when in orbit. Now with parallax, rotation, and mod support!

## P.U.M.P.
P.U.M.P. (Prevent Unwanted Manual Pump-placement) takes out the chore of making outposts. It's a selection tool with which you can select liquid resources (oil, sulfuric acid, etc.. ) and it then proceeds to plan the layout of pumpjacks and pipes for you.

## Mining Patch Planner
Adds a planner that designs a mining drill layout for a resource patch.

## Smart Deconstruction Planner
Instantly create a deconstruction planner filtered to the hovered entity for fast, precise cleanup.

## Rate Calculator
Calculate maximum production and consumption rates for the selected machines.

## Visible Planets Scale
Adds automatic scaling to planets rendered by Visible Planets in Space based on their gravity and increases or reduces the rotation speed based on their day/night cycle duration.

## Bullet Trails
Adds bullet trails to machine gun type bullets. Works with gun turrets, guns, vehicle-mounted guns, and anything else that uses that type of ammo.

## Logistic Helper
Effortlessly streamline your Factorio logistics with Logistic Helper! Use an easy shortcut to drag over an area and automatically configure chest-inserter-assembler conditions based on recipes, making mall setup and logistics management a breeze across your entire factory.

## Train Trails
Trains create colorful glowing trails wherever they go

## Automatic Train Painter
Automatically colors locomotives and wagons based on their contents.

## Automatic Station Painter
Automagically colors stations based on the trains' colors.

## Color whole train
Colors all train wagons, not only locomotives, based on the destination train stop. Works with cybersyn.

## Fluid Wagon Color Mask
Adds a color mask for the fluid wagon.

## Rainbow Asteroid Collectors
Fix the """fix""" in 2.0.25 where asteroid collector tentacles have less colourful rainbow effect on them.

## Caramelldansen Labs
Turns your Research Labs into a Rave with a touch of Caramelldansen

## Enhanced Shadows
Replaces commonly used assets containing baked-in shadows with shadow-separated variants.

## Rainbow Character
Automatically cycles player colors through the rainbow and other color themes

# Overview

The Starstruck modpack is a unique constellation of mods that subtly enhance gameplay, without fundamentally changing it. The modpack includes many small visual mods, quality-of-life tools, and other enhancements that can be added or removed at any time without disrupting the factory.

# Included Mods

### Visuals & Aesthetics

- [__Rainbow Menu Logos__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/rainbow-menu-logos) - Recolors the factorio logo in the main menu.
- [__UwUbe__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/uwube) - Makes the Wube logo go UwU.
- [__Nicer Fuel Glow for Space Age__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/NicerFuelGlow-Fixed) - Adds a subtle tint to the glow of burner entities based on their fuel type.
- [__Heat Glow__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/heat_glow) - Prettier heat glow.
- [__Enhanced Shadows__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/enhanced-shadows) - Replaces baked-in asset shadows with shadow-separated variants.
- [__Bullet Trails__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/bullet-trails) - Adds bullet trails to machine gun type bullets.
- [__Fluid Wagon Color Mask__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/FluidWagonColorMask) - Adds a color mask for the fluid wagon.

### Visuals & Aesthetics: Rainbows

- [__Rainbow Character__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/rainbow-character) - Automatically cycles player colors through the rainbow and other color themes.
- [__Disco Thrusters__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/DiscoThrusters) - Brings the party to your spaceship with disco thrusters that light up in rainbow colors.
- [__Rainbow Lasers__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/RainbowLasers) - Makes laser beams RGB! Works on laser turrets and personal laser defenses.
- [__Disco Science__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/DiscoScience) - Science labs light up with the colors of the science packs they're consuming.
- [__Rainbow Asteroid Collectors__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/RainbowCollector) - Fixes the reduced rainbow effect on asteroid collector tentacles from 2.0.25.
- [__Train Trails__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/train-trails) - Trains create colorful glowing trails wherever they go.

### Tools & Automation

- [__Rate Calculator__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/RateCalculator) - Calculate maximum production and consumption rates for the selected machines.
- [__Automatic Train Painter__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Automatic_Train_Painter) - Automatically colors locomotives and wagons based on their contents.
- [__Automatic Station Painter__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/automatic-station-painter) - Automagically colors stations based on the trains' colors.
- [__Color whole train__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/color_whole_train) - Colors all train wagons based on the destination train stop. Works with cybersyn.
- [__Automatic Underground Pipe Connectors__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/automatic-underground-pipe-connectors) - Automatically places a pipe when two undergrounds are placed at a corner or one space apart.
- [__Filter Helper__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/FilterHelper) - Adds context-aware quick select for filter inserters.
- [__P.U.M.P.__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/pump) - Planner tool that designs pumpjack and pipe layouts for fluid resource patches.
- [__Mining Patch Planner__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/mining-patch-planner) - Planner tool that designs mining drill layouts for resource patches.
- [__Smart Deconstruction Planner__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/smart-deconstruction-planner) - Instantly creates a deconstruction planner filtered to the hovered entity.
- [__Cutscene Creator__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/cutscene-creator) - Adds a command to create custom cutscenes. Use /cutscene and shift-click to specify waypoints.

### Statistics & Monitoring

- [__Preserve Products Produced__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/preserve-products-produced) - Preserves "Products finished" stat on machines when upgraded. Also preserves turret damage and kills.
- [__Milestones__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Milestones) - Track your progress by finding out how fast you created key items. Compare with friends or challenge yourself.
- [__Better Victory Screen__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/better-victory-screen) - Shows interesting game statistics upon victory, like total hand-crafting time and peak power production.

### Environment & Ambiance

- [__Caramelldansen Labs__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/CaramelldansenLabs) - Turns your research labs into a rave with a touch of Caramelldansen.
- [__Brake Squeal__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/brake-squeal) - Makes trains squeal/screech as they brake, dynamically changing based on speed.
- [__Glowing Trees__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/glowing_trees) - Adds a glowing aura to trees and bioluminescence for foliage and decoratives.
- [__Subtle Lighting__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/dim_lamps) - Modifies lamp intensity, creating a more gradual falloff to darkness.
- [__Leave The Lights On__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/leave_the_lights_on) - Your vehicle's headlights stay on after you exit.
- [__Dirt Path__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Dirt_Path) - Heavily trafficked areas change over time.
- [__Mining Scars__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/MiningScars) - Depleting ore patches causes significant scarring of the map.
- [__RP Item Anarchy__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/rp_item_anarchy) - Creates item sprite variations so you can watch individual items move through the factory.
- [__Visible Planets in Space__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/visible-planets) - Renders the local planet behind platforms when in orbit. With parallax, rotation, and mod support.
- [__Visible Planets Scale__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/vp-scale) - Adds automatic scaling to planets based on gravity and adjusts rotation speed based on day/night cycle.


### Miscellaneous
- [__trainsaver__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/trainsaver) - A dynamic screensaver that follows trains as they drive around.
- [__Pick It Up__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/pickitup) - When mining an inserter, also mines items it dropped on the ground. Miniloader support.

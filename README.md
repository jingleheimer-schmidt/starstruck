# Overview

The Starstruck modpack is a unique constellation of mods that subtly enhance gameplay, without fundamentally changing it. The modpack includes many small visual mods, quality-of-life tools, and other enhancements that can be added or removed at any time without affecting .

# Included Mods

### Visuals & Aesthetics

- [__Rainbow Menu Logos__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/rainbow-menu-logos) - Recolors the factorio logo in the main menu.
- [__UwUbe__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/uwube) - Makes the Wube logo go UwU.
- [__Nicer Fuel Glow for Space Age__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/NicerFuelGlow-Fixed) - Tints burner entity glow based on fuel type.
- [__Heat Glow__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/heat_glow) - Enhances the glow effect on heatpipes
- [__Enhanced Shadows__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/enhanced-shadows) - Replaces baked-in asset shadows with shadow-separated variants.
- [__Bullet Trails__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/bullet-trails) - Adds visual trails to machine gun type bullets.
- [__Fluid Wagon Color Mask__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/FluidWagonColorMask) - Adds a color mask for the fluid wagon.
- [__Rainbow Character__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/rainbow-character) - Animates player colors with rainbow colors and other themes.
- [__Disco Thrusters__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/DiscoThrusters) - Animates rocket thruster exhaust with rainbow colors.
- [__Rainbow Lasers__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/RainbowLasers) - Animates laser beams with rainbow colors.
- [__Disco Science__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/DiscoScience) - Science labs glow with the colors of the science packs they're consuming.
- [__Rainbow Asteroid Collectors__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/RainbowCollector) - Adds a rainbow colors to asteroid collector tentacles.
- [__Train Trails__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/train-trails) - Adds colorful trails to the trains.

### Tools & Automation

- [__Rate Calculator__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/RateCalculator) - Adds a planner tool to calculate maximum production and consumption rates.
- [__Automatic Train Painter__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Automatic_Train_Painter) - Colors locomotives and wagons based on their contents.
- [__Automatic Station Painter__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/automatic-station-painter) - Colors stations based on the trains' colors.
- [__Color whole train__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/color_whole_train) - Colors train wagons based on the destination train stop.
- [__Automatic Underground Pipe Connectors__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/automatic-underground-pipe-connectors) - Places a pipe when two undergrounds are placed at a corner or one space apart.
- [__Filter Helper__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/FilterHelper) - Adds context-aware quick select for filter inserters.
- [__P.U.M.P.__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/pump) - Adds a planner tool that designs pumpjack and pipe layouts for fluid resource patches.
- [__Mining Patch Planner__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/mining-patch-planner) - Adds a planner tool that designs mining drill layouts for resource patches.
- [__Smart Deconstruction Planner__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/smart-deconstruction-planner) - Easily summon a deconstruction planner filtered to the hovered entity.
- [__Cutscene Creator__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/cutscene-creator) - Adds a command to create custom cutscenes.

### Statistics & Monitoring

- [__Preserve Products Produced__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/preserve-products-produced) - Preserves "Products finished" stat on machines when upgraded. Also preserves turret damage and kills.
- [__Milestones__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Milestones) - Tracks progress by sharing how fast you created key items.
- [__Better Victory Screen__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/better-victory-screen) - Shows additional interesting game statistics upon victory.

### Environment & Ambiance

- [__Caramelldansen Labs__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/CaramelldansenLabs) - Plays Caramelldansen music in research labs.
- [__Brake Squeal__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/brake-squeal) - Makes trains squeal/screech as they brake, dynamically changing based on speed.
- [__Glowing Trees__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/glowing_trees) - Adds a glowing aura to trees and bioluminescence for foliage and decoratives.
- [__Subtle Lighting__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/dim_lamps) - Reduces the intensity of lamps.
- [__Leave The Lights On__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/leave_the_lights_on) - Vehicle's headlights stay on after you exit.
- [__Dirt Path__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Dirt_Path) - Heavily trafficked terrain change with wear
- [__Mining Scars__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/MiningScars) - Depleting ore patches significantly scars the map.
- [__RP Item Anarchy__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/rp_item_anarchy) - Adds scale and offset for item sprite variations.
- [__Visible Planets in Space__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/visible-planets) - Renders the local planet behind platforms when in orbit. With parallax, rotation, and mod support.
- [__Visible Planets Scale__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/vp-scale) - Adds scaling to visible planets based on gravity.


### Miscellaneous
- [__trainsaver__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/trainsaver) - Adds a dynamic screensaver that follows trains as they drive around.
- [__Pick It Up__](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/pickitup) - When mining an inserter, also mines items it dropped on the ground.
